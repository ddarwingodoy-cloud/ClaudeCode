Hiring Manager Interview — Darwin (16/6)

Experienced, transparent, and easy to talk to. Diogo brings one of the deepest affiliate backgrounds we've seen for this role.

Around seven years at YOLO Group (Coingaming) in the UK — from customer support and fraud control into sports betting operations and affiliate management — then a year and a half at Better Collective as a Key Account Manager focused on Brazil, and most recently affiliate roles at GG7 (Play7) and Stake. He's sat on both the advertiser and publisher sides, which gives him an unusually complete view of how operators and affiliates work together, plus a strong, current Tier 1 network in the Brazilian market.

He's hands-on with MyAffiliates (tracking, deal setup, creative management) and TG Lab, so he could pick up the operation with little ramp. His commercial read is mature: he favors Tier 1 affiliates on hybrid CPA + RevShare contracts, prioritizing sustainable, long-term deals over short-term volume, and was deliberate about managing a R$150–200 CPA range to protect margin. He also shares the post-World Cup consolidation read.

My main hesitation is on fit rather than capability: I didn't come away feeling he was genuinely motivated or energized by this specific challenge. At this build-from-scratch stage, that drive matters as much as the experience, and it's where I landed.

Recommendation: strong on knowledge and network, but I would not advance him — the motivation and energy this stage demands weren't there in the conversation.
