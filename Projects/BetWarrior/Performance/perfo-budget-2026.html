Methodology & Assumptions
CPA Curve Logic
Jun ($330K) and Jul ($310K) peak for Copa do Mundo (Jun 11-Jul 19). Post-Copa budget redistributed across Aug-Dec to produce a harmonic descent: $80 (Aug), $76 (Sep), $71 (Oct), $66 (Nov), $60 (Dec), roughly $4-5 per month. May anchor at $195.37 is actual per Diego tracking (27/05). Total Aug-Dec envelope preserved at $914,199.
FTD Distribution Source
Monthly FTD targets derived from JP Forecast (Simulator - New). Performance uses the ex-affiliates seasonality index (row 8) applied to the annual total of 19,729 FTDs. August dip reflects post-Copa normalization; Dec peak reflects end-of-year acquisition push.
Open Items
CPA targets are projections pending confirmation of Jan–Apr actuals by Diego (current estimate: $2,302,468 consumed). Targets should be revised if the actual consumed differs materially. CR improvement post-Copa (Aug onward) will be quantified when data is available.
