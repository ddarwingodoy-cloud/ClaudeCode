For context · Paid Social did respond

To be clear, this is not about the whole media operation. Paid Social scaled ~3x its May level (≈72% of the traffic lift) on new Cup creatives, the channel did its job. The gap is Search, which captures existing demand and does not depend on the creative pipeline.

What our view can’t tell us

The blind spot

GA4 shows the outcome (sessions), not the bid. We cannot tell if the zero is coverage, bid/rank or auction loss, that lives in Google Ads, which BR does not have access to.

  • Lost IS by budget → demand slipping due to spend, and we may have budget to cover it.
  • Lost IS by rank → bids / quality score, something we optimize together.

What we are asking, to close the gap together

  • Access: read access to Google Ads & Business Manager (or a recurring report), so BR can see its main acquisition source and answer most of this without asking.
  • Impression Share on the Cup campaigns: lost to budget vs lost to rank.
  • The keyword basket we run for the Cup: brand-defense, match-specific, long-tail.
  • Budget pacing: with Google at ~50% vs ~57%, can we free up or redirect for this window?
