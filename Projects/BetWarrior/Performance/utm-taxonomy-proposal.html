01 · Context
BetWarrior BR's budget is structured around 6 acquisition channels: Performance, Affiliates, Influencers, Brand & Media, Organic, and Direct. The current UTM taxonomy — built for Argentina's media mix — does not expose this structure in GA4. As a result, channel-level reporting requires manual source mapping outside the analytics tool, creating inconsistency and blind spots.

This document proposes a taxonomy adjustment that preserves Juanca's campaign naming conventions while adding a channel layer that aligns GA4 data directly with the BR budget distribution.

P1
Medium is not a reliable channel signal

Google campaigns appear under both cpc (auto-tagged) and paid_media (manual), splitting the same channel into two rows. Taboola appears as paid_media and referral. No single filter isolates "all paid media."

P2
Affiliates carry no partner identity

Source values are product categories (deportes, casino) instead of affiliate names. It is impossible to break down affiliate performance by partner in GA4 without cross-referencing MyAffiliates separately.

P3
Influencers are invisible or misclassified

publisher / social_paid identifies some influencer traffic but hides the influencer name. Influencer creatives (e.g., int_imagen_tourinho) appear inside Meta paid campaigns, indistinguishable from performance ads.

P4
Brand & Media has no unified tag

Sofascore uses branding as medium. No standard exists for other sponsorships, display awareness, or third-party publishers. Each partner invents their own pattern.

P5
Channel-level reporting requires manual assembly

There is no GA4 dimension that maps directly to the BR budget channels. Every report requires a lookup table to group sources into channels — error-prone and not scalable as the channel mix grows.

03 · Proposed Taxonomy
The core change: utm_medium becomes the channel identifier, matching BR's budget distribution categories. Each medium value maps 1:1 to a budget line. utm_source retains its role as the platform or partner name. Campaign and content naming conventions remain largely unchanged.

04 · What Changes
Before

GA4 filter for "Affiliates"
Must manually include: deportes, casino, retargeting, (not set) where medium = affiliate

Google traffic
Appears as google/cpc AND search/paid_media AND pmax/paid_media — 3 rows for the same channel

Influencer identity
Hidden inside creative name (int_imagen_tourinho) or lumped as publisher/social_paid

Channel report
Requires manual source-to-channel mapping table, rebuilt every time a new partner is added

After

GA4 filter for "Affiliates"
Single filter: medium = affiliates — all partners included automatically

Google traffic
Single row: source = google, medium = perfo — auto-tag conflict resolved via GA4 data import or manual override

Influencer identity
Visible at source level: source = tourinho, medium = influencer

Channel report
Native GA4 segment by medium — no mapping table needed, new partners automatically classified
