Context
Why We Need a New Framework
BetWarrior Brazil operates with six budget channels: Performance, Affiliates, Influencers, Brand & Mídia, Organic, and Direct. Each has distinct investment levels, CPA targets, and optimization logic. The current UTM structure was not designed with this channel breakdown in mind, making it impossible to attribute GA4 sessions and conversions to the right budget line.

Current State
Five Structural Problems
1 Configuration not aligned with budget structure
The current setup does not allow reading acquisition data in GA4 according to the 6 budget channels. Spend and FTDs cannot be compared on the same axis without manual reconciliation.
google appears under Perfo, Brand and Organic with no separation
2 Influencers and affiliates unmapped
These two channels represent ~20% of BR budget but appear as generic sources in GA4 "link", "bio", or simply (not set). No reliable FTD measurement.
utm_source=link · utm_medium=(not set)
3 Naming inconsistency
The same channel appears under different capitalizations and spellings, fragmenting sessions in GA4 and making aggregation unreliable.
Google / google / Google_Ads / google-ads
4 Brand and paid media mixed
Brand campaigns (TV, OOH, sponsorships) share utm_source values with paid digital, making it impossible to isolate brand investment impact from direct response.
utm_source=meta · (brand video + perf ad)
5 No campaign naming convention
Campaign names are free-form across teams. The same product/audience combination has dozens of variations, blocking automated reporting.
br_maio_ftd / BRmayFTD / brasil-may-acquisition

Proposed Taxonomy
Parameter Structure
The framework uses four mandatory parameters and one optional. Every paid source must be tagged before going live. The utm_source identifies the budget channel directly. The utm_medium identifies the specific platform or partner within that channel, enabling drill-down without losing the channel grouping.
Parameter Purpose Req. Example Values
utm_source Identifies the budget channel — the source of the spend Mandatory perfo, afil, influ, brand
utm_medium Identifies the specific platform or partner within the channel Mandatory google, meta, player7777, danilo_lavieri, uol
utm_campaign Identifies the campaign with structured naming convention Mandatory perfo_ftd_google_may26
utm_content Identifies the creative or ad variant Recommended banner_300x250, video_30s_v1
utm_term A/B test label or keyword Optional teste_A, apostas_esportivas
Campaign Naming Convention
All paid campaigns follow the pattern: [channel]_[objective]_[medium]_[period]
• perfo_ftd_google_may26  •  influ_awareness_lavieri_may26  •  afil_ftd_player7777_may26

Next Steps
Implementation Plan
Four steps to full implementation. Weeks 1–2 cover alignment and documentation; weeks 3–4 cover rollout and validation. No historical data needs to be touched.
1
Align taxonomy with Juanca
Review this document together. Confirm utm_source values for each channel, particularly affiliate IDs and influencer handles. Agree on campaign naming convention and enforcement by owner (Juanca for Performance, Darwin for Affiliates, Influencers and Brand).
May 28, 2026 – Juanca in São Paulo
2
Publish UTM guide and URL builder
Create a shared Google Sheet with a URL builder and a reference tab listing all approved source/medium/campaign values per channel. Mandatory for all new campaigns from June onward.
Week of June 2
3
Configure GA4 Channel Groups + Google Ads override
Create a custom channel grouping in GA4 mapping standardized utm_source values to the 6 budget channels. Critical: enable "Allow manual tagging (UTM values) to override auto-tagging" in GA4 Admin → Google Ads Links. Without this, Google Ads injects a gclid parameter that overwrites utm_campaign in GA4, making campaign-level data unreliable.
Week of June 2 – data team
4
Retroactive audit and migration
Identify active campaigns still using old UTM strings. Prioritize high-spend first (Performance, Affiliates). Update tracking links in platform ad managers. Flag any campaigns that cannot be migrated mid-flight for the next cycle.
June: Juanca (Perfo) + Darwin (Affiliates, Influencers, Brand)
Suggested Rollout: Start with Performance only
To de-risk the rollout, we propose starting with Performance campaigns: Google, Meta, TikTok, Taboola, Verve. This covers the largest budget channel (~30% of total spend). Affiliates, Influencers and Brand follow in week 2 of June, coordinated by Darwin.
What we are NOT changing
• PowerBI tracking (separate from UTM)
• Affiliate platform tagging (MyAffiliates) UTM is supplemental
• Historical data applies to new campaigns only
• GA4 property / event configuration
